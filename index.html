Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Click any question below to view answers about our fresh organic mushrooms, pure spawn, and hardwood pellets.

help What is mushroom spawn?
expand_more

Mushroom spawn is the biological seed (pure mycelium cultured on sterilized grain) used to inoculate growing substrates. Organic Anabe supplies certified 1st-generation laboratory Oyster and Milky spawn across Karnataka at ₹130/KG (Min 10 KG).

help Where can I buy oyster and milky mushroom spawn in Karnataka, and what is the price?
expand_more

You can buy 1st-generation laboratory-certified Oyster Mushroom Spawn (Pleurotus ostreatus) and Milky Mushroom Spawn (Calocybe indica) directly from Organic Anabe (ಆರ್ಗ್ಯಾನಿಕ್ ಅಣಬೆ). Both varieties are priced at ₹130 per KG with a 10 KG minimum order quantity (MOQ). Packed in microporous 0.2-micron filter bags and delivered statewide across Mysuru, Bengaluru, Mandya, Hassan, and all 31 districts.

help What are all the types of pellets available at Organic Anabe?
expand_more

Organic Anabe manufactures and distributes all types of pellets: 1) 100% natural Broadleaf Hardwood Sawdust Pellets (Oak, Beech) for gourmet mushroom cultivation substrates; 2) High-calorific Biomass Fuel Wood Pellets (~4,500 kcal/kg) for industrial boilers, furnaces, and heating; and 3) Screened Mixed Sawdust Pellets. Available in 100 KG bags, 500 KG jumbo bags, and commercial metric tonne truckloads.

help Can hardwood pellets be used for mushroom farming?
expand_more

Yes! 100% pure broadleaf hardwood sawdust pellets provide an optimal, chemical-free substrate for cultivating high-yield Oyster, Shiitake, and Milky mushrooms. Available in 100 KG bags to bulk tonnes across Karnataka.

For Mushroom Spawn (both Oyster and Milky), the minimum order quantity is 10 KG (which can be split 50:50 between Oyster and Milky upon request). For Pellets, the minimum order is 100 KG in heavy moisture-protected sacks, scalable up to commercial truckloads.

The Story Behind Our Farm

Founded in 2026 by three cousins, Organic Anabe began with a shared passion for sustainable agriculture and fresh, healthy food. Repurposing an existing shelter, we transformed the space into a state-of-the-art mushroom cultivation facility that merges modern farming technology with age-old organic principles.

eco
Our commitment is simple: 100% natural, zero chemicals.

We cultivate our mushrooms on premium, sustainable substrates made from paddy straw and hardwood pellets, creating the ideal natural environment for robust growth and rich flavor. By combining precise environmental controls with zero-chemical methods, we deliver pure, nutrient-dense mushrooms straight from our farm to your table.

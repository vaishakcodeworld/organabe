Coastal Oyster Mushroom Sukka

ಕರಾವಳಿ ಸಿಂಪಿ ಅಣಬೆ ಸುಕ್ಕ
Serves 3-4

Tender shredded oyster mushrooms dry-roasted in freshly ground byadgi chillies, roasted coconut, and fragrant curry leaves.

menu_book Key Botanical Ingredients Heritage Prep
  • 200g Fresh Oyster Mushrooms
  • 1/2 cup Freshly grated coconut
  • 4-5 Roasted Byadgi chillies
  • Coriander, cumin & fennel
  • Fresh curry leaves & onion
  • Cold-pressed coconut oil / ghee
Step 1: Dry roast whole spices & chillies; coarsely grind with grated coconut.
Step 2: Sauté curry leaves and onions in coconut oil until translucent golden.
Step 3: Add shredded oyster mushrooms; cook on high heat 4 mins as moisture evaporates.
Step 4: Toss with coarse spiced coconut mixture and finish with fresh lime.

Smoky Charcoal Milky Seekh Kebab

ಸ್ಮೋಕಿ ಹಾಲು ಅಣಬೆ ಸೀಖ್ ಕಬಾಬ್
Serves 4

Minced meaty milky mushrooms blended with soft cottage paneer, crushed coriander seeds, mint, and green chillies skewered and flame-grilled.

menu_book Key Botanical Ingredients Tandoor Special
  • 250g Fresh Milky Mushrooms
  • 100g Grated soft Malai Paneer
  • 2 tbsp Roasted Gram flour (Besan)
  • Crushed Coriander & Cumin seeds
  • Fresh mint & green chillies
  • Pure cow butter for basting
Step 1: Coarsely mince steamed milky mushrooms and squeeze excess moisture.
Step 2: Knead with grated paneer, roasted besan, crushed spices, and fresh herbs into dough.
Step 3: Press onto skewers and sear over smoking grill pan or tawa, basting with butter.
Step 4: Slide off skewers, dust with tangy chaat masala, and serve with onion rings!

Dum Pukht Milky Mushroom Biryani

ಹಾಲು ಅಣಬೆ ದಮ್ ಬಿರಿಯಾನಿ
Serves 4-5

Layered saffron basmati rice and thick, juicy chunks of milky mushrooms slow-cooked in a clay pot with mint, caramelized onions, and spices.

menu_book Key Botanical Ingredients Royal Dum
  • 300g Fresh Milky Mushrooms
  • 2 cups Aged Basmati rice
  • Saffron milk & caramelized onions
  • 1 cup Hung curd + ginger garlic
  • Fresh mint sprigs & cow ghee
  • Shahi whole garam masala
Step 1: Blanch sliced milky mushrooms in warm turmeric water for 2 mins; drain well.
Step 2: Marinate in spiced hung curd, mint, and ginger-garlic paste for 30 minutes.
Step 3: Par-boil fragrant basmati rice with whole spices to 70%.
Step 4: Layer mushrooms and rice in handi, top with saffron ghee, seal and dum-cook for 22 mins.

Pan-Seared Garlic Thyme Oyster

ಗಾರ್ಲಿಕ್ ಬಟರ್ ಸಿಂಪಿ ಅಣಬೆ ರೋಸ್ಟ್
Serves 2-3

Flash-seared in golden cold-pressed butter or olive oil with crushed malabar peppercorns, fresh green thyme, and toasted garlic cloves.

menu_book Key Botanical Ingredients Gourmet Skillet
  • 200g Fresh Oyster Mushroom clusters
  • 2 tbsp Pure Cow Butter / Olive oil
  • 6-8 Crushed garlic cloves
  • Fresh garden thyme sprigs
  • Coarse Malabar black pepper
  • Flaky sea salt & lemon zest
Step 1: Separate oyster mushrooms into natural fan clusters without cutting.
Step 2: Sear dry on a sizzling heavy skillet for 2 minutes to concentrate flavor.
Step 3: Add butter and crushed garlic; baste as the edges turn golden crisp.
Step 4: Toss with fresh thyme sprigs and freshly cracked black peppercorns.

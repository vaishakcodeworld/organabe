What is Spawn in Mushroom Farming? Pure Mycelium Carrier & Cultivation Science

In mycology and commercial mushroom agriculture, mushroom spawn (ಅಣಬೆ ಬೀಜ) is the pure carrier medium—sterilized cereal grains such as whole sorghum (jowar), wheat, or millet—densely permeated by active, 1st-generation fungal mycelium. It acts as the vegetative seed used to inoculate mass agricultural substrates like sterilized paddy straw or hardwood sawdust pellets. Sourcing certified 1st-generation laboratory spawn guarantees maximum mycelial vigor, zero weed-mold contamination (such as Trichoderma or Aspergillus), and high biological yield.

biotech How to Store Mushroom Spawn Correctly

Store your spawn bags in a clean, sanitized, dark room or refrigerator at 4°C to 10°C for Oyster spawn and 15°C to 20°C for Milky spawn. Never expose spawn to direct sunlight or temperatures above 35°C. Inoculate into prepared substrate within 7–14 days of delivery for peak mycelial vigor.

calculate How Much Spawn Do You Need?

The standard commercial spawning rate is 3% to 5%. For every 100 KG of wet pasteurized straw or hydrated hardwood pellets (approx. 35 KG dry material), you will require 3 to 5 KG of mushroom spawn. Our 10 KG minimum order supports 200–300 KG of wet substrate, generating ~100–140 KG of fresh harvest.

water_drop How to Hydrate Pellets for Mushroom Cultivation

Place 10 KG of dry hardwood pellets into a sterilized tub or filter patch bag. Add 14 to 15 liters of boiling water (140% hydration ratio). The pellets break down within 10 minutes into fluffy, steam-pasteurized sawdust substrate. Allow to cool to room temperature before mixing with our ₹130/KG Oyster or Milky spawn.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) • Karnataka Mushroom & Pellets Guide

help What is spawn in mushroom farming?

Spawn in mushroom farming is the pure vegetative mycelium carrier (usually sterile cereal grains such as whole sorghum, wheat, or millet) that functions as the biological "seed" to inoculate bulk growing substrates. High quality 1st-generation laboratory grain spawn guarantees rapid colonization, vigorous yield, and zero contamination. Organic Anabe supplies lab-grade Oyster and Milky mushroom spawn across Karnataka at ₹130/KG.

help What are all the types of pellets available at Organic Anabe?

Organic Anabe manufactures and distributes all types of pellets: 1) 100% natural Broadleaf Hardwood Sawdust Pellets (Oak, Beech) for gourmet mushroom cultivation substrates; 2) High-calorific Biomass Fuel Wood Pellets (~4,500 kcal/kg) for industrial boilers, furnaces, and heating; and 3) Screened Mixed Sawdust Pellets. Available in 30 KG bags, 500 KG jumbo bags, and commercial metric tonne truckloads.

help How do you use hardwood pellets as a substrate with mushroom spawn?

Hardwood pellets are easily prepared by adding 1.4 to 1.5 liters of boiling water per 1 KG of dry pellets (pasteurization). Within 10 minutes, the pellets expand into fluffy, contaminant-free sawdust. Once cooled below 28°C, inoculate with 3% to 5% pure Oyster or Milky mushroom spawn (30–50 grams spawn per 1 KG wet substrate) for complete colonization in 12–15 days.

How to Hydrate Pellets for Mushroom Cultivation

1. Place 10 KG of dry hardwood pellets into a clean container or filter patch bag.
2. Add 14 to 15 liters of boiling water (140% hydration ratio) for steam pasteurization.
3. Within 10 minutes, the pellets expand into fluffy, contaminant-free sawdust substrate.
4. Allow the substrate to cool below 28°C before mixing with our ₹130/KG Oyster or Milky Mushroom Spawn.

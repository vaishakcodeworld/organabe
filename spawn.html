What is Spawn in Mushroom Farming? Pure Mycelium Carrier & Cultivation Science

In mycology and commercial mushroom agriculture, mushroom spawn (ಅಣಬೆ ಬೀಜ) is the pure carrier medium—typically sterilized whole cereal grains such as sorghum (jowar), wheat, or millet—densely permeated by active, 1st-generation fungal mycelium. It acts as the vegetative seed used to inoculate mass agricultural substrates like sterilized paddy straw or hardwood sawdust pellets. Sourcing certified 1st-generation laboratory spawn guarantees maximum mycelial vigor, zero weed-mold contamination (such as Trichoderma or Aspergillus), and high biological yield.

Mushroom Spawn Cultivation FAQ

Commercial spawning rate is 3% to 5% of wet substrate weight. For 100 KG of wet pasteurized straw or hydrated hardwood pellets (approx. 35 KG dry material), you require 3 to 5 KG of mushroom spawn.
